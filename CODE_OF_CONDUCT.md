# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Discuss the code, not the person.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including sexualised language.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and to public
spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/vertusdesign/deskpins-for-mac/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may edit, hide or reject contributions, and may ban contributors, for behaviour
they consider inappropriate.
